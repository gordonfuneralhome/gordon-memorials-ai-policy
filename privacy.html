Gordon Memorials AI Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026

Gordon Memorials AI is an internal research and reporting application operated by Gordon Funeral Home in Durant, Oklahoma. Questions about this policy may be sent to gordonfuneralhome@gmail.com.

Information processed

The application organizes publicly available obituary, funeral service, cemetery, and grave-marker research. Records may include names, obituary dates, funeral homes, cemetery information, and links to public sources. Public research may be incomplete or unverified.

Google account access

With authorization from the account owner, the application uses the Google Gmail API permission https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send to send internal research reports from the authorized Gmail account to designated recipients. The application does not request Gmail inbox reading, message deletion, or general mailbox management permissions.

Use and sharing

Information is used for internal research and report delivery. The application does not sell Google user data or use Gmail account data for advertising. Report contents are shared with the recipients designated by the account owner and processed by Google to deliver the emails.

Storage and security

Research records are stored in a private GitHub repository. Gmail authorization credentials are stored as protected GitHub Actions secrets, not published on this site. Access is restricted to authorized operators. No internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

Retention and deletion

Research records are retained for internal operational needs and may be corrected or deleted by the operator. Gmail authorization may be revoked by the account owner through Google Account permissions. Requests concerning information held by this application may be sent to the contact email above.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the application's features change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
